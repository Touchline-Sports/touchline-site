GPS load and injury-risk alerts

Who's one hard session from a hamstring.

Import the GPS export your club already produces. Touchline works out each player's acute:chronic workload ratio, tracks it against their own four-week baseline, and alerts you to anyone drifting out of range before it becomes an injury.

Catapult, STATSports or a CSV you built yourself. You map the columns once and they stay mapped.

Answers go straight to your coaching and medical staff. Compliance is the whole game here, which is why it fits on one screen.

Privacy

The physio's notes stay with the physio.

Every club asks this, usually second. Here is the answer before you have to.

RoleCan see
PlayerTheir own data only. Their rank and the squad average, never a teammate's numbers.
CoachThe whole squad's load, wellness flags and availability. Not medical notes, not diagnoses.
PhysioThe squad, plus injury records, rehab plans and return-to-play stages.
Club adminUsers, consent records and billing. Not medical notes.
The leagueAggregated totals only. Nothing about any individual player.

Enforced in the database, not hidden in the interface. Players under 18 have nothing collected until a parent or guardian consents, and that consent can be withdrawn at any time. Data is stored in Australia.

For leagues and federations

One picture of the whole competition.

When your clubs run Touchline, your league gets fixtures and ladders across every grade, player statistics by age group, a team of the week picked from the team sheets rather than a committee, and a fantasy competition that puts families in your league's news every week.

Each club keeps its own data. The league sees counts, never a player's medical record.
